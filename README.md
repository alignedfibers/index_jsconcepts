# General Web

**Shawn Stark | Selected examples of work, 2008–2026**

This site gathers examples of work and study from different periods of my life, rather than documenting one continuous project. Together they show how I have approached problems, explored unfamiliar subjects, developed ideas, and carried them into practical work. Across the years, the collection reflects curiosity, analytical judgment, independent learning, and the ability to communicate and apply what I have learned. It is a sample of that history, not its full extent.

## Periods represented

- **2008 — Early foundation:** A concentration of hands-on learning, planning, structured information, and interactive design work.
- **2009 — Heaviest documented period:** Extensive study, research, analysis, written explanations, and practical technical work spanning several fields and kinds of problems.
- **2010 — Independent exploration:** Continued experiments, problem-solving, and sharing what I learned through technical writing and demonstrations.
- **2017–2020 — Later conceptual work:** A separate collection of JavaScript studies, brought together in 2017 and maintained in the source history through 2020, examining how programs behave and how ideas can be tested.
- **2026 — Archive assembly:** Earlier material gathered and organized here for exploration and comparison; this is the collection date, not the original creation date of the work.

After extracting the site, open `index.html` to browse the examples locally.
